# Features

The iOS, Android and web apps share one backend, one data set and one core feature set. This page is a tour of what they do. Where a platform differs on purpose, see [Platform Differences](/features/platforms).

## Races

A season calendar with an "up next" banner, sprint-weekend badges and per-race results.

- Switch between Race, Qualifying, Sprint and Practice classifications.
- Each result row shows gap to leader, grid delta (places gained or lost) and points.
- Non-finishers show their status, such as "DNF", "+1 Lap" or "Accident".
- Each race shows the weekend schedule converted to your device's time zone.

![The Races tab on iOS, showing the season calendar](/images/ios/ios-schedule.png){.doc-img .md}

## Race Replay

Scrub or play through a race lap by lap and watch the running order animate.

- Position-change arrows, tyre compounds and lap times on every row.
- Transport controls: scrub to any lap, jump to the first or final lap, or step five laps at a time.
- Adjustable speed: 0.5×, 1×, 2× or 4×.

## Drivers

A searchable roster with headshots, numbers and team colours.

- Search by name, code or number.
- Star a driver to pin them to the top of the list. Favourites are stored on the device and do not sync, since there are no user accounts.
- Driver detail shows a season form sparkline and every result.
- **Head-to-head:** pick two drivers and compare points, wins, podiums, poles, best finish, DNFs and their direct race and qualifying records.

![The Drivers tab on iOS](/images/ios/ios-drivers.png){.doc-img .md}

## Constructors

Standings-ordered team cards with driver line-ups and team liveries. Team detail adds the full roster.

## Standings

Driver and constructor championships with gap-to-leader bars and win counts.

- **Progress:** a round-by-round chart of cumulative championship points.
- **Reliability:** season finish rate and DNF-by-cause bars for each driver and team.

![Championship standings on iOS](/images/ios/ios-standings.png){.doc-img .md}

## Circuits

Season venues in calendar order, each tagged Raced or Upcoming. Tap a raced circuit for a detail page with the track map, length, corner count, fastest lap and podium, plus quick actions to open the replay or full results.

![A circuit's track map on iOS](/images/ios/ios-track-map.png){.doc-img .md}

## Race analysis

Every completed race opens a grid of deep-dives: telemetry, lap times, tyre strategy, qualifying, weather, retirements, flags and the full race-control log. See [Race Analysis](/features/analysis).

## Session reminders

The iOS and Android apps can schedule local notifications before sessions.

- Remind me: the day before, one hour before, or 15 minutes before.
- For these sessions: practice, qualifying, sprint, race.

The web app leaves reminders out. See [Platform Differences](/features/platforms#web).
